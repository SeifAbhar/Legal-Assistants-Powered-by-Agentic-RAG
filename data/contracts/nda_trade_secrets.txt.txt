CONTRACT TYPE: Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement
PARTIES: Enterprise Sales Corp. (Discloser) and Departing VP of Sales (Receiver)
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2020-06-01
---
1. Confidential Information Defined. “Confidential Information” includes all customer lists, pricing structures, sales pipeline data, and any non-public business information disclosed to Receiver during the course of employment.
2. Obligations. Receiver shall not, during or after employment, disclose, copy, or use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the benefit of Discloser.
3. Remedies. In the event of breach, Discloser may seek injunctive relief, monetary damages, and attorney's fees.
